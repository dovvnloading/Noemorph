## Executive finding

The catalog is now broad enough to support disciplined collection work, but it is not yet defensibly close to exhaustive. The immediate problem is not only missing names. It is uneven record depth, fragmented taxonomy, and the absence of normalized evidence, method, geography, language, population, and measurement fields.

Of 577 records, 172 (29.8%) lack at least one of `claim_scope`, `boundary_note`, `evidence_profile`, or `open_questions`. Several of those are canonical anchors—GWT, GNWT, HOT, Attention Schema Theory, Dynamic Core, predictive-processing approaches, phenomenal/access consciousness, and intentionality. Adding hundreds of peripheral rows before completing these anchors would increase visual and semantic noise without producing a more trustworthy map.

The correct sequence is:

1. Normalize identities and controlled fields.
2. Complete the core-profile tranche and pending citation review.
3. Add source-backed missing named theories/models after identity resolution.
4. Add constructs, measures, and contexts as distinct record types.
5. Build taxon-, geography-, language-, and clinical-boundary dossiers.
6. Only then review semantic candidates and begin synthesis.

## The taxonomy currently overstates usable diversity

The catalog has 299 distinct domain strings, of which 247 are singletons, and 422 tradition strings, of which 385 are singletons. Catalog status is even more fragmented: 433 distinct values, 417 used once. In practice these fields are partly prose rather than controlled vocabularies. That makes filters brittle, inflates apparent variety, and makes cross-group coverage comparison unreliable.

The first schema migration should therefore introduce controlled identifiers plus display labels for domain, tradition, status, record type, evidence target, method, population/system, measurement, geography, source language, and source position. Original strings should be retained for migration audit. Parent/variant/version relationships are also essential: PCI-ST should enrich the existing PCI family, not become a duplicate theory.

## Evidence and method coverage

The 577 records contain 1,209 citation assignments pointing to 1,004 unique sources. Citation verification is 680 DOI-resolved, 174 human-verified, 276 locator-recorded, and 79 pending metadata review. Seventy-five records are affected by at least one pending citation; 332 records have every citation DOI-resolved or human-verified. These are locator-integrity measures, not support-strength measures.

Source use is very shallow: 916 of 1,004 sources appear on only one record, while only 13 appear on five or more. This breadth is useful for discovery, but source overlap cannot yet carry substantive weight. Claims need source locators and an explicit support/challenge/neutral stance before relationships can be inferred.

There is no normalized research-method field. Keyword signals find conceptual/formal analysis in 363 records, behavioral/task paradigms in 220, historical/textual/ethnographic work in 202, clinical/diagnostic methods in 125, computational/mathematical methods in 113, and systematic/meta/consensus work in 79. More operationally specific signals are sparse: self-report/interview/experience sampling 30, EEG/MEG/electrophysiology 24, MRI/PET/fNIRS 15, perturbation/stimulation 34, and pharmacology/anesthesia 40. Because these are recursive keyword hits, a record can mention a method without containing direct evidence from it. They are gap indicators, not coded study designs.

## Intellectual geography

No record-level geography field exists. A conservative intellectual-provenance coding was therefore limited to the 112 Historical tradition records: MENA 27, Europe/Mediterranean 25, South Asia 24, East Asia 17, Africa/African diaspora 5, Buddhist transregional/unresolved 4, Tibetan/Himalayan 3, Indigenous North America/Arctic 3, Latin America/Caribbean 2, Southeast Asia 2, and Pacific/Aboriginal Australia 0.

This does show meaningful breadth beyond a Euro-American canon, especially in MENA, South Asian, and East Asian material. It also identifies a serious remaining imbalance. Pacific and Aboriginal Australian, Southeast Asian, Latin American/Caribbean, Indigenous American, and sub-Saharan African traditions need commissioned dossiers with language, translator, community, and interpretive provenance. A decontextualized one-row summary would reproduce the coverage problem rather than solve it.

## Missing named theories and models

These are identity-screened proposals, not automatic additions. Each still requires curator confirmation against names, aliases, and conceptual neighbors.

### P0

**Hypnosis and hypnotic agency.** The catalog's altered-state material does not adequately represent Neodissociation, Dissociated Control, Cold Control, Response Expectancy/Response Set, or sociocognitive/integrative social-cognitive accounts. A recent systematic review distinguishes 25 theories across 10 categories, demonstrating that this is a real family-level gap rather than a single missing row ([systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2024.103730); [sociocognitive/dissociation comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21644121/); [Cold Control](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2009.10.007)).

**Self-awareness and anosognosia.** Add identity review for Pattern Theory of Self and the Cognitive Awareness Model. These are not substitutes for minimal/narrative-self constructs: one is an integrative self-pattern framework, while the other generates clinically bounded hypotheses about impaired awareness ([Pattern Theory](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2013.00443/full); [Cognitive Awareness Model](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2012.09.006)).

**General cognitive architectures.** A catalog of theories of mind should include bounded records for ACT-R, Soar, CLARION, EPIC, Sigma, and Leabra. These must be labeled architectures of cognition, not promoted by default to theories of phenomenal consciousness ([ACT-R](https://act-r.psy.cmu.edu/); [Soar](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(87)90050-6); [CLARION](https://doi.org/10.1080/09528130701191560); [EPIC](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327051hci1204_4)).

**Social and interactive mind.** Social Baseline Theory, the Interactive Alignment Model, Relational Frame Theory, and the Double Empathy Problem fill distinct gaps in social regulation, dialogue, relational learning, and reciprocal misunderstanding. The Double Empathy Problem belongs as a challenge/context record, not as a mechanism of consciousness ([Social Baseline](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1751-9004.2011.00400.x); [Interactive Alignment](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X04000056); [Double Empathy](https://doi.org/10.1080/09687599.2012.710008)).

**Neural reuse.** Massive Redeployment and Neural Reuse are important architectural constraints on mapping cognitive functions to neural systems and provide a corrective to one-region/one-function assumptions ([Massive Redeployment](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-007-9233-2); [Neural Reuse](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20964882/)).

### P1

The next tranche should identity-review Monitor and Acceptance Theory, Mindfulness-to-Meaning Theory, the Reperceiving/Intention-Attention-Attitude model, Pivotal Mental States, Social Simulation Theory of Dreaming, Mental Models Theory of reasoning, domain-general Dual-Process accounts, Reggia's computational machine-consciousness program, Gamez's MC1–MC4 taxonomy, Synthetic Phenomenology, and Thalamocortical Dysrhythmia. Boundaries matter: dream generation, dream function, and dream consciousness are different targets; machine-consciousness taxonomies are not evidence that current systems are conscious; thalamocortical dysrhythmia is a clinical mechanism, not a general consciousness theory.

## Measures, constructs, and context must not be mislabeled as theories

The catalog should add or deepen four separate families:

- **Measures:** PCI-ST as a PCI variant; amplitude and synchrony coalition entropy; Motor Behavior Tool-Revised; SECONDs; SWADOC; Coma/Near-Coma and Loewenstein Communication scales; hypnotizability instruments.
- **Methods:** Descriptive Experience Sampling, micro-phenomenological interviewing, Experience Sampling Method, and species-appropriate comparative paradigms.
- **Constructs:** hypnotic agency/involuntariness/suggestibility; pivotal or plasticity-enhanced mental states; multidimensional animal-consciousness profiles; cognitive-motor dissociation.
- **Context/evidence dossiers:** per-taxon animal evidence, AI indicator implementation and welfare uncertainty, clinical boundary/confounder records, and adversarial theory testing.

For measurement, [PCI-ST](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brs.2019.05.013) and coalition-entropy work ([open-access article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4529106/)) illustrate why a measure hierarchy is necessary. For clinical practice, the [AAN disorders-of-consciousness guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30089618/) provides a boundary framework, not another theory. For first-person methods, [Descriptive Experience Sampling](https://doi.org/10.1080/14780880903009274) and [micro-phenomenology](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11097-006-9022-2) should be represented as methods with reliability and interpretation limits.

Animal consciousness needs taxon-specific evidence dossiers rather than one generic node. The [Dimensions of Animal Consciousness](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2020.07.007) framework argues against reducing the question to a single scale, while the [LSE cephalopod and decapod review](https://www.lse.ac.uk/asset-library/news/sentience-in-cephalopod-molluscs-and-decapod-crustaceans-final-report-november-2021.pdf) demonstrates the depth of evidence coding needed per taxon.

Artificial-consciousness records likewise need three layers: theory-derived indicators, implementation evidence, and welfare/moral-uncertainty context. The indicator report ([Butlin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)) is a framework for assessment, not a verdict.

## Existing records that should not be duplicated

The identity screen confirmed that the catalog already includes AIR, Entropic Brain, Integrated Information Decomposition, Dynamic Core, Neural Darwinism, IWMT, Unlimited Associative Learning, Neurobiological Naturalism, Self-Model Theory, PCI, major bedside disorders-of-consciousness scales, anesthesia/sedation indices, Lempel-Ziv complexity, weighted symbolic mutual information, local-global and fMRI motor-imagery paradigms, no-report paradigms, infant markers, the Cambridge and New York declarations, Dimensions of Animal Consciousness, theory-derived AI indicators, and ConsScale.

This matters because a title-only audit would incorrectly report many of these as missing. Identity resolution must specifically guard against false collisions such as Epicurean/EPIC, Shared Mental Models/Mental Models Theory, and Dual-Process Recognition Memory/domain-general Dual-Process theory.

## Synthesis gate

Semantic similarity can generate review candidates, but it cannot create established relationships. The 2025 adversarial IIT/GNWT collaboration should be stored as a preregistered prediction-and-result matrix with direct claim citations, including negative and ambiguous outcomes, rather than as a winner-take-all edge ([Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1)). The same rule should apply to every proposed novel connection: bibliographic identity review first, substantive claim review second, and no graph promotion without cited evidence and an explicit human disposition.

The measurable acceptance criteria before synthesis are therefore: controlled-field migration complete; the P0 core tranche fully profiled; pending citation records resolved or explicitly quarantined; duplicate/variant identities reviewed; claim-level citation representation operating; and semantic candidates separated from accepted catalog relationships.

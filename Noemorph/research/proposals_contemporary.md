# Contemporary consciousness expansion: source and rationale report

Date: 2026-08-30  
Scope: bounded acquisition screen for empirical/computational consciousness research, clinical measurement, altered states, comparative sentience, and artificial-consciousness work, with emphasis on 2020–2026.

## Result

The acquisition packet contains **27 named candidates**. Twenty-six are candidate catalog objects and one—COGITATE—is deliberately typed as an **evidence-profile update only**, not a theory or standalone catalog record. A stricter promotion packet contains **five new-record candidates** with at least two distinct authoritative/primary works plus **two COGITATE profile updates** for the existing GNWT and IIT records.

Files:

- `research/proposals_contemporary.json`: all 27 acquisition proposals, including one-source rows that require more sourcing before promotion.
- `research/promotion_contemporary.json`: five promotion-gated new rows plus GNWT/IIT evidence additions; it is not registered in `catalog.json`.

No result below is a claim of scholarly novelty. “Missing” means only that the checked catalog files did not contain the candidate as an exact record name or alias.

## Audit and method

The audit parsed 12 `data/catalog*.json` files: 578 raw rows and **577 unique IDs**. Candidate names and aliases were compared against the existing name/alias inventory. The final 27 candidates have zero exact-name collisions. Close conceptual neighbors were then inspected manually so that a version, scale, or protocol was not mistaken for a new theory.

Source priority was:

1. defining peer-reviewed paper or formal publication;
2. primary validation study or official versioned program page;
3. independent validation or authoritative review used to bound claims;
4. primary preprint only when the object is itself a recent preprint-level governance proposal.

DOI metadata were cross-checked against Crossref; selected clinical and scale records were also checked in PubMed/PMC or on an official institutional page. This caught and corrected several misleading secondary-source author strings. Authors are retained per work; PIQ and PIS, in particular, remain separate instruments with separate teams.

## Promotion gate

The catalog kind allowlist is: `Scientific theory`, `Model or framework`, `Clinical / state model`, `Theory of mind / social cognition`, `Phenomenological construct`, `Philosophical position`, `Argument / challenge`, and `Historical tradition`.

All five new rows in `promotion_contemporary.json` map to `Model or framework`, matching how the current catalog types clinical scales, psychometric families, protocols, and machine-consciousness tests. All use `review_state: provisional` and the catalog verification allowlist.

## Important non-merges

- **IIT 4.0 ≠ generic IIT ≠ PCI.** The version has its own formal objects; PCI and PCI-ST are tractable response-complexity measures, not Φ-structure computations.
- **MCI ≠ empirical-support tally.** MCI asks how theory-central and cross-theory-portable a measure is.
- **SECONDs / CRSR-FAST / MBT-r ≠ CRS-R.** Each is a separate tool with separate population, purpose, and validation limits.
- **PIQ ≠ PIS.** They have different author teams, timings, item structures, and validation programs.
- **3D-ASCr is a scale revision**, not a theory and not a replacement of all legacy APZ/5D/11D data.
- **LuCiD ≠ signal verification ≠ TDI ≠ Dream Engineering.** These are, respectively, a report scale, a physiological verification family, a specific incubation protocol, and an umbrella engineering agenda.
- **Theory-light ≠ eight-criteria ≠ Edge of Sentience.** These are a comparative research strategy, a taxon-evidence rubric, and a normative precaution framework.
- **ACT ≠ Dung's naturalness constraint ≠ Bilateral Turing Test ≠ theory-derived architectural indicators.** They target different evidential objects.
- **AI-governance proposals are not AI-consciousness evidence.** They address uncertainty and institutional preparation.

## Open acquisition work

The 21 one-source rows should not enter the reviewed catalog until a second nonduplicate source has been attached and assessed. Priority work is:

1. independent or multisite validations for ECI, PCI-ST, CRSR-FAST, MBT-r, and newer psychometric instruments;
2. prospective predictive and measurement-invariance studies for psychedelic insight, connectedness, integration, INOE, and 3D-ASCr;
3. independent closed-loop dream-incubation replication with blinded report coding;
4. independent applications of MCI and the comparative sentience frameworks;
5. operational tests of machine-consciousness proposals that explicitly audit training contamination, gaming, and substrate assumptions;
6. claim-level COGITATE ingestion without turning a mixed result into a binary theory score.

This batch improves contemporary coverage while preserving the project's sequencing rule: collect and validate source-bounded records first; infer semantic connections only after coverage and review gates are satisfied.

# Noemorph consciousness and mind seed catalog

Research snapshot: 2026-08-29. This is a citation-first seed catalog, not a claim of exhaustiveness or an adjudication of truth. Robert Lawrence Kuhn's 2024 *Landscape of Consciousness* is the broadest organizing starting point used here, supplemented by systematic/scoping reviews, primary papers, and scholarly reference works.

## Scope rules

- **Kinds are not interchangeable.** `CT` = theory/model intended to explain consciousness; `MP` = metaphysical position about mind/consciousness; `NF` = neural/cognitive framework or proposed correlate that is not by itself a complete theory; `MF` = methodological framework; `HT` = historical or living philosophical tradition; `SC` = theory/model of mindreading or social cognition (the psychological “theory of mind” literature). A row can have two labels where the literature genuinely uses it in both ways.
- **Statuses are deliberately non-binary.** `major-active` = prominent active research program, not “proven”; `active-minority` = active but less central; `contested` = substantial live dispute; `speculative` = limited direct empirical support or weakly testable; `historical` = primarily historically influential; `framework` = useful organizing method not a standalone consciousness explanation.
- **Citations are provenance, not endorsements.** Primary sources state the view; review/reference sources establish how it is classified. A theory's appearance here says nothing about its truth.
- **No relationship synthesis yet.** During collection, only explicit variant/family membership should be stored. Cross-theory influence, compatibility, contradiction, and synthesis edges should wait for a later, source-by-source evidence pass.

## Anchor surveys and taxonomies

1. Robert Lawrence Kuhn, “A landscape of consciousness: Toward a taxonomy of explanations and implications,” *Progress in Biophysics and Molecular Biology* 190 (2024), 28–169. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pbiomolbio.2023.12.003) — exceptionally broad taxonomy; intentionally inclusive and non-adjudicative.

5. Itay Yaron et al., “The ConTraSt database for analysing and comparing empirical studies of consciousness theories,” *Nature Human Behaviour* 6 (2022), 593–604. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-021-01284-5) and [open database](https://contrastdb.tau.ac.il/) — empirical study/theory provenance.

## Recommended data model for citation integrity

Each **theory/position record** should contain: `canonical_name`, `aliases`, `kind`, `scope_target` (state, content, phenomenal character, access, self, unity, function, ontology, evolution), `summary_neutral`, `originators`, `era`, `domain`, `status`, `version`, `citation_confidence`, and `review_state`.

Do not attach prose claims directly to a theory record. Create a separate **claim record** with: `claim_text`, `claim_type` (definition, mechanism, prediction, implication, criticism, historical attribution), `stance` (asserts, supports, challenges, reports), `source_id`, `locator` (page/section/figure), `quoted_or_paraphrased`, `reviewer`, and `verified_at`.

Sources should be immutable **source records** with DOI/PMID/ISBN/SEP URL, full bibliographic metadata, source tier (`primary`, `systematic-review`, `scholarly-reference`, `commentary`), access URL, and retraction/correction status. Author identities need separate authority records (ORCID where available) to prevent name blending.

Only after a coverage threshold is met should the system create **relationship assertions**. Each edge (`variant_of`, `influenced_by`, `compatible_with`, `contradicts`, `extends`, `empirically_compared_with`) must itself be a cited claim, not an inference from tags. UI graph layout proximity must never be presented as evidence of conceptual similarity.

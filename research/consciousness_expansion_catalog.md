# Noemorph consciousness expansion catalog

This second-pass humanities expansion continues the seed numbering at rows 100–144. Inclusion records a historically or academically relevant account; it does not endorse the account or imply empirical confirmation. Each row retains exactly two source locators and uses a normalized research domain.

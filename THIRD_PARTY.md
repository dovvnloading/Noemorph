# Sources and third-party rights

Noemorph cites research and points readers to original publications. Citation
does not transfer ownership or permission to reproduce a source. The MIT license
applies to original project material contributed under that license; it does not
relicense cited papers, websites, trademarks, datasets, models, or other
third-party material. Their respective terms continue to apply.

The repository deliberately excludes local copies of Kuhn's 2024 paper and its
extracted text, as well as the scraped Closer To Truth discovery registry, which
contains source-page text. Obtain and use any optional source material under its
own terms; do not commit it or replace the exclusions with bulk copies.
The reviewed catalog includes citations, source-derived descriptions, and
structured research metadata. Contributors should use original wording, retain
attribution and precise source links, and flag any questionable copied material
for review. Inclusion in an index is not endorsement or independent verification.

The project depends on third-party Python and JavaScript packages. Their licenses
are supplied by the respective packages and should be reviewed when redistributing
dependencies or bundled builds.
